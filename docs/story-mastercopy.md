# Staffroom Review — Story Mastercopy / Human-Readable Index

This is the editorial-facing index of the canonical story registry.

**Canonical machine source:** `data/story-registry.js`  
**Verification utility:** `lib/story-verification.js`  
**Removed/archived stories:** `docs/story-archive.md`

The registry contains one master record per underlying story. A story can have multiple page placements without becoming multiple master records. Distinct newsletter/blog packages may remain separate records when they are genuinely separate editorial products.

## How to read this index

- **ID** is permanent and should never be reused.
- **Primary** is the story's main editorial home.
- **Also appears** lists additional presentation locations.
- **Status** is the editorial state, not a quality judgement.
- **URL** is the real article URL when published; blank means no dedicated article URL exists yet.
- Current page cards may be placeholders and may only point to their containing page; those presentation links are not treated as canonical article URLs.
