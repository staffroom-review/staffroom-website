# Staffroom Review — Homepage Reference Mapping: The Ken 1–7

## Purpose

This is the ordered structural map for the supplied The Ken homepage reference.

It is **not a literal clone contract**. It defines the compositions Staffroom Review must reconstruct closely while replacing subject matter, branding and imagery with Staffroom material.

## Global rules

- Preserve the reference sequence and major composition types.
- Preserve comparable page depth and information density.
- Preserve variation between feature-led, list-led, collection-led and image-led sections.
- Do not flatten the page into a repeated card grid.
- Do not remove existing Staffroom story headings/deks to make the page shorter.
- Additional Staffroom modules may be inserted later, but they must use the same visual system.

## Section 1 — Opening

Use:
- left editorial story without requiring a large image
- dominant central image
- dominant central headline/dek immediately associated with image
- right supporting story package
- strong horizontal alignment between the three areas

The centre is the visual anchor.

## Section 2 — Feature + support

Use:
- large image-led feature on the left
- large serif headline/dek below the image
- right column containing a text-led story followed by a smaller image story
- dotted/thin internal separator where appropriate

## Section 3 — Recommendation / discovery

Use:
- section heading plus long accent rule
- multiple vertical columns
- first item in each column may have an image
- remaining stories become compact text entries
- high density and short reading intervals

This section must feel materially denser than the feature sections.

## Section 4 — Central feature

Use:
- left text-led story
- central dominant image with large headline/dek beneath
- right text-led story plus supporting image story
- clear internal separators

## Section 5 — Multi-column collection

Use:
- repeated narrow columns
- short category/series headings
- one image-led lead item per column where appropriate
- several compact stories beneath
- thin separators
- faster scanning rhythm

## Section 6 — Large feature chapter

Use:
- section heading/rule
- left text-led feature
- central dominant image and headline/dek
- right text-led story plus image-led story
- substantial whitespace around the dominant composition

## Section 7 — Closing feature chapter

Use:
- section heading/rule
- dominant feature treatment
- asymmetric supporting stories
- strong image-to-headline relationship
- enough vertical depth to transition naturally into the retained footer

## Content preservation

The current homepage's existing story material must remain available to these modules, including its existing headlines, deks and future-story concepts.

Use additional content from `content-seed.md` when the reference density requires more slots.

## Responsive rule

On mobile, preserve the reference reading order rather than mechanically shrinking desktop columns.

Typical transformation:
1. section heading/rule
2. dominant feature
3. supporting stories in reference reading order
4. compact list
5. next section

Tablet should interpolate these relationships without becoming a separate page.
